Privacy Policy — Neon Clone Rush

  • Effective date: October 14, 2026
  • Developer: Aydın Works
  • Contact: aydinworks.dev@gmail.com

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Neon Clone Rush on Android (package com.aydinworks.neonclonerush), a game published by Aydın Works ("we", "us").

The short version: the game has no accounts, asks for no personal details and has no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows advertising; the adverts are delivered by Google, and Google's advertising software processes some device information to do that. Both parts are described below.

1. Information the game itself handles

We do not collect, receive or store any information about you. Aydın Works operates no server for this game and receives no data from it.

The game saves the following on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • your game progress: coins, stars, unlocked levels, best scores, purchased upgrades and which tutorial hints you have seen;
  • counters the game uses for its own pacing, such as how many runs you have started and won;
  • your sound settings.

This data never leaves your device through the game. It is not linked to your identity. The game does not ask for your name, e-mail address, age, contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera, and it does not create an account for you. There is no sign-in and no cloud save: the game does not copy your progress to any server of ours or of anyone else.

Deleting it. Uninstalling the game, or choosing Settings › Apps › Neon Clone Rush › Storage › Clear storage on your device, permanently deletes everything the game has saved. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing further for us to delete. Depending on your device settings, Android's own backup service may include app data in your device backup with Google; that is controlled in your device's backup settings, not by the game.

Analytics. The game contains no analytics, attribution, crash-reporting or social SDK of ours or of any third party, other than what is part of Google's advertising software described in section 2.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, and asks for advertising consent, where the law requires it, with Google's User Messaging Platform. These are the only third-party software in the game that processes information from your device. (A test build of the game distributed without ads does not contain this software, and section 2 does not apply to it.)

The game shows two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads, which play only when you choose to watch one, to continue a run you have just lost (offered at most once per run);
  • Interstitial ads, which may appear occasionally after a lost run, before the next one starts: never during play, never in your first runs of a session, and not more often than once every three minutes.

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK — not Aydın Works — collects and processes information from your device. According to Google's published disclosure for the SDK, this includes:

InformationWhat it is
IP addressYour device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (not precise) location
Device and advertising identifiersThe Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device
Product interactionsFor example app launches, taps and video views, including interaction with ads
Diagnostic informationApp and SDK performance data, such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you; we see aggregated advertising statistics (such as how many ads were shown and what they earned) in our AdMob account.

Ads may be personalised (based on your interests) or non-personalised, depending on your choices, your region and your device settings. How Google uses information from apps that use its services is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices about advertising

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required). The first time you start the game, a consent message provided by Google (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choices about the use of your data for advertising. No ads are requested until you have answered. You can change your answer at any time in the game under Settings › Privacy choices.
  • US state privacy laws. Where applicable, the same Privacy choices entry lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for advertising.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device settings: Settings › Google › Ads (the exact path differs by device).
  • Not watching rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are always optional. The game is fully playable without them.

3. Permissions

The game requests no access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, files or phone. It uses network access and the advertising ID permission, which Google's advertising software needs to load ads.

4. Children

Neon Clone Rush is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help as far as we are able, bearing in mind that we do not hold the data ourselves.

5. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information and we share none, because we do not collect any. Information processed by Google for advertising is handled by Google under its own policies, as described in section 2.

6. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete it (see section 1). We hold no personal data and therefore retain none. Information processed by Google's advertising software is retained by Google under its own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy), and you can exercise your advertising choices in the game as described in section 2. You can always contact us with any question, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy (for example, if a new feature handles information differently), we will update this page and its effective date before or when the change is released.
